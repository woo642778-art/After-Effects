## Decisions already made

- Phase number equals major version: Phase 5 may publish only `5.0.0 (5)` and `After-Effects-5.0.0-unsigned.ipa` after completion.
- Internal engine modules retain the `Vertex` namespace.
- Platform-neutral models may not expose AVFoundation, Metal, Core Image, MetalPetal, VideoIO, UIKit, or SwiftUI objects.
- AVFoundation remains the media backend behind `VertexMediaAVFoundation`.
- Native Metal is the Phase 4 render backend behind `VertexRenderMetal`.
- MetalPetal remains an audited, unlinked future effect/composition candidate.
- VideoIO remains deferred until timed preview or video export.
- VideoLab remains a design reference, not a time, media, project, or render foundation.
- MiniCut remains GPL behavioral reference only.
- Preview and output must continue to share one semantic render graph.
- Exact time, explicit color metadata, recoverable persistence, source traceability, and honest status cannot be weakened.
- Build workflow uses push/manual execution and ignores documentation-only changes to avoid checksum loops.
