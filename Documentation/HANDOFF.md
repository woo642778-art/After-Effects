## Decisions already made

- Internal engine modules may keep the `Vertex` namespace while the installed product uses the After Effects identity.
- iOS 17 remains the minimum deployment target for the current build. Do not introduce iOS 18-only primitives without availability boundaries.
- MetalPetal is the leading GPU-processing candidate, hidden behind a Vertex adapter.
- VideoIO is the leading AVFoundation preview/export utility candidate, hidden behind an adapter.
- VideoLab is a design reference, not the core time or project engine.
- MiniCut is GPL-3.0 and may be studied for behavior only unless the licensing strategy changes.
- Preview and export must share the same semantic render graph.
- Exact time, recoverable persistence, explicit color metadata, and license traceability cannot be weakened for speed.
- Commercial effect suites such as Sapphire and BCC are behavior and quality references only; the project will implement independent algorithms and names.
- Documentation-only commits do not rebuild the IPA workflow.
