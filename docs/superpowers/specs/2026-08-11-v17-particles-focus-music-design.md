# Vertex2 17.0 Particles, Procedural Graphics, and Focus Music Reliability

## Scope

Vertex2 17.0 builds on the verified V16 effects-expansion head. It delivers three user-visible changes together because they share preview/export qualification and iPad release packaging: a larger executable effect catalog, the Phase 17 particles/procedural-graphics roadmap, and reliable offline Focus Music.

## Effects and V17 graphics

The executable effect catalog grows from 99 to 113 effects by adding fourteen Vertex-owned native effects:

- Vertex Particle Emitter
- Vertex Particle Trails
- Vertex Particle Burst
- Vertex Particle Snow
- Vertex Fractal Noise
- Vertex Turbulence Field
- Vertex Procedural Grid
- Vertex Procedural Rays
- Vertex Procedural Dots
- Vertex Plasma
- Vertex Voronoi
- Vertex Checker
- Vertex Gradient
- Vertex Star Field

All fourteen run through the same preview/export effect resolver. Particle/procedural frames are generated by a deterministic Metal compute pipeline. The renderer receives the exact composition RationalTime already carried by CompositionEffectRequest. Its seed, time, and parameter inputs fully determine the output frame, so preview and export do not rely on mutable wall-clock simulation state.

The particle modes expose birth density/rate, lifetime, size, speed, gravity, turbulence, hue/intensity, and seed controls. The analytic simulation includes point/radial emitters, velocity and acceleration, deterministic turbulence, bounded collision reflection, procedural sprite shapes, and sampled trails. Burst and snow are distinct simulation presets rather than renamed copies.

Procedural modes use the same seeded Metal generator for fractal/value noise, turbulence, grids, radial rays, dot fields, plasma, Voronoi cells, checker patterns, gradients, and stars. This avoids copyrighted third-party shaders/assets and makes the output reproducible.

## Focus Music

Focus Music remains completely separate from project/timeline audio and is never exported. The existing six synthesized tracks expand to at least twenty-four original Vertex-generated instrumental loops with distinct tempo, root, progression, chord quality, brightness, texture, and pluck character.

The current AVAudioSession `.ambient` policy is replaced by `.playback` with `.mixWithOthers`. This prevents Focus Music from being silently suppressed by silent-mode style policies while still allowing other app/system audio to coexist. AVAudioPlayer preparation and `play()` success are checked and surfaced as errors rather than optimistically toggling `isPlaying`.

No commercial songs or copyrighted recordings are bundled. Tracks are generated locally as deterministic PCM/WAVE data.

## Qualification

Release requires:

1. Portable Swift tests pass with exactly 113 executable descriptors and valid defaults.
2. All fourteen V17 GPU/procedural effects render on iPad Simulator at realistic video size.
3. Re-rendering the same V17 effect at the same RationalTime produces byte-identical PNG output.
4. Focus Music catalog contains at least 24 unique original tracks; generated data opens in AVAudioPlayer as stereo audio.
5. Focus Music playback policy is `.playback` + `.mixWithOthers`.
6. Existing V16 effect-render regression, AI model audit, app tests, and unsigned Release build remain green.
7. The packaged 17.0.0 IPA passes bundle/version/device/architecture/signing/model/Metal audits and receives a SHA-256 sidecar.
